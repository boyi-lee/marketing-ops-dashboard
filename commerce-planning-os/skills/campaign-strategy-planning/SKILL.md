# Campaign Strategy Planning
1. Read inventory-health, product-lifecycle, merchandise-profitability and kpi-gap-analysis outputs; require snapshot IDs and metric versions.
2. Diagnose KPI constraint before selecting a promotion; consider no-discount options.
3. Examine seven references: business diagnosis, consumer motive, assortment, promotion economics, timing/channel, scenarios and learning.
4. Exclude stock-out items, unconfirmed inbound, missing costs and prohibited brand promotions.
5. Compare at least three materially distinct strategies including a non-discount option; do not sum uplift without overlap adjustment.
6. Run deterministic scenario and profitability tools; never invent forecast or incremental effect.
7. Save a platform-local draft with assumptions, evidence, risks, data version, forecast confidence and reviewer status.
8. Never mutate Shopify or approve the plan on behalf of a person.
